# Indian ad craft (sources in docs/research/indian-ads.md and docs/plan/02-templates-and-hooks.md)

## Anatomy (every template, every pasted script)
1 Hook (0–2.5 s / 0–3 s): a question, pain, number, dare, before/after, POV or festival moment; text card from frame 1; product or face visible. 2 Setup. 3 Proof (product in use, 2–3 shots). 4 Offer (one concrete fact and a deadline: "500 g box, delivery by Diwali"; no price or discount % until owner decision D11). 5 CTA + end card (WhatsApp / call / visit / order; number held ≥ 2 s).
Budgets: ~2.5 Hindi words/s; a cut every 2–4 s; no shot over 5 s; product by second 3 and in ≥ 50 % of shots; brand presence (logo-only frames) under 25 % of runtime.

## The "feels like an ad" checklist (scorecard)
Text hook ≤ 0.5 s and spoken hook ends ≤ 2.5 s · hook is a library type, not a description · product in first frame and ≥ 50 % of shots · same face in all people shots · voice energy follows beats · offer is one concrete fact with a deadline · an Indian CTA with the end card ≥ 2 s · captions on, one script system, inside the safe box · music ducked, hits on cut 1 and offer · cut every 2–4 s, no dead air · brand colours, logo in last 3 s · no garbled text / extra fingers / drift · campaign cue when in Campaign mode · AI label when required.

## Hooks (16 types)
Relatable pain ("Kya aapke bhi…"), question, price shock (parked until D11), bold claim, before/after (never clinics), POV, pattern interrupt, stop-scrolling, social-proof number (true only), curiosity/myth-buster, visual-only, text-card, festival FOMO, city shout-out, sceptic's twist (presenter, never a fake customer), 3-second challenge. Sound hooks are an SFX hit, never licensed/trending audio in a paid ad.

## No money on the website
The owner's rule: no cost, price, fee or monetary value anywhere on the website, including generated ads previewed there. Templates, hooks and sample content carry no prices; the `price-anchor` template is parked until D11.

## Platform safe box (1080×1920)
Text, logos, CTA inside x 65–1015 and y 270–1250 (satisfies Meta's unified zone, top 14 % / bottom 35 % / 6 % sides, and Shorts, top 10 % / bottom 25 % / right 10 %). Burn captions in (FB Reels ads have no auto-captions). WhatsApp file ≤ 16 MB.

## Compliance gates (block = no override)
- ASCI synthetic-content guidelines (29 Sep 2026, in force ~Dec 2026): label synthetic presenters/voices/realistic generated settings ("Video created using AI"); **prohibited even if labelled**: fabricated testimonials, exaggerated AI results/transformations, fake locations, deepfakes/likeness without consent, AI authority figures.
- MeitY IT Rules 2026 (20 Feb 2026): prominent label, audio prefix for a cloned voice, provenance metadata kept.
- CCPA 2022/2023: discounts show the original price (so no discount claims while the website shows no prices, D11); no false urgency; no drip pricing; no celebrity name/image without permission; no "free" with hidden conditions.
- NMC (6 Oct 2026, doctors/clinics): no testimonials, before/after, "best/No. 1/painless", offers; AI content carries an "AI-generated" mark. Coaching (CCPA 2024): no "100 % selection", toppers only with written consent. Jewellers: 6-digit HUID. Real estate: RERA number + QR. Food: no "100 % pure" without proof.
- Disclaimers: same language as the claim, ≤ 2 lines, each held > 4 s, under the CTA.
