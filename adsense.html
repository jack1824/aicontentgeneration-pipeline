one ad, end to end

A render’s journey

  1. Idea lands in the pill — “story ad for my dental clinic…” — and rides to /plan.
  2. Gemini returns 3 directions: pipeline, director-style shots, narration script. You pick and edit.
  3. Preflight: pod alive? models present? Only then are ElevenLabs credits spent on the voice.
  4. GPU renders each shot (queue position shown live); clips download home as they finish.
  5. FFmpeg assembles: stitch → narration timed over the cut → music ducked beneath the voice.
  6. End card stamps brand · tagline · offer — the only place text is ever typed, never generated.
  7. Optional polish (chunked enhance) — and the final lands in the Library, ready to post.
