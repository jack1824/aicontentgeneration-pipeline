The senior architect's critique was right in DIRECTION and lands on our
CURRENT CODE. Corrected, verified render rules for the episode compiler:

1. NEVER flip a recurring hero between engine families. Today the hero
   flips Wan-S2V(speaking) <-> LTX(everything else), held by anchor TEXT
   ONLY on LTX beats, with ZERO keyframe carry-over. Fix — keep the hero
   in the Wan 2.2 family:
     speaking hero beat  -> Wan S2V (reference face image + ElevenLabs audio)
     SILENT hero beat    -> Wan i2v from the locked keyframe  (NOT S2V —
                            S2V is hard audio-driven, cannot do silent shots;
                            NOT LTX-text-only)
     b-roll / environment -> LTX (native audio); the room plate locks the room
   Reason it matters: cross-engine = a render/color/texture SEAM between
   decoders (not a "latent identity reset"); identity is anchored by the
   REFERENCE, not the engine.

2. Feed a reference IMAGE into LTX hero beats too (IC-LoRA Ingredients),
   not anchor text alone — today LTX hero identity is text-only.

3. Add a Wan-2.2 identity adapter on hero beats — promote from "eval spike"
   to MVP. CORRECT tools for Wan VIDEO (NOT PuLID/IP-Adapter/InstantID —
   those are SDXL/Flux IMAGE-only, a dead end on Wan):
     Stand-In  (official Wan 2.2 weights now, single frontal face, ~1% params)
     Lynx      (ByteDance, Apache-2.0, single-image, benchmark-leading ~0.78)
     Phantom   (up to 4 refs, multi-subject/product)
   Per-character Wan LoRA stays the strongest DURABLE tier for cross-episode
   (bakes identity into weights); adapters are the no-train fallback and
   COMPOSE on top.

4. Keyframe composite (Qwen-Edit: face + room plate -> per-beat first frame)
   + FLF2V are INTRA-SHOT seam tools only. Do NOT architect episode
   consistency around latent/frame "carry-over" — it does not exist across
   episodes (ephemeral per-run tensors, new pod = zero shared state). Anchor
   cross-episode identity on the STORED ASSET PACK re-injected every run:
   hero portrait + IC-LoRA reference sheet + character LoRA + Qwen keyframes.

5. KEEP the measurement layer (P3) — the senior's note has no way to VERIFY
   identity held. Standard + commercially-licensable: AuraFace FaceSim (faces)
   + DINOv2/CLIP VBench-style (rooms/look), reported together (each metric
   has a documented bias). This is a genuine addition, not invented.

CFG=1 distilled LTX note: real cost is fine DETAIL + negatives inert + can't
raise CFG — NOT temporal instability (low CFG is actually more stable).
Identity at CFG=1 rides on IC-LoRA strength (~1.4) + Qwen keyframes + NAG.

4 pods / serverless = THROUGHPUT, not consistency (each shot still independent).
Our template IS the "shared identity context across pods" the senior wants —
as long as every worker gets the stored ASSET PACK, not just prompts+seeds.

SHOW TEMPLATES — EPISODIC AD SYSTEM: THE PLAN
Researched 2026-07-20 (11-agent deep dive: Higgsfield teardown,
competitor landscape, consistency tech, multi-speaker, 2 codebase
maps + 4 gap-fills on enforcement/licensing/cost/reproducibility)
Status: AWAITING APPROVAL — no code until approved.
====================================================================

THE ONE-LINER
  A Show Template = cast + rooms + look, locked ONCE and validated.
  An Episode = template + a new script. Same teacher, same classroom,
  same student faces — in Ep2, Ep5, or three weeks from now.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT THE RESEARCH SETTLED
--------------------------------------------------------------------

HIGGSFIELD (as asked):
- Presets = a CURATED library (50-70 camera moves, ~50 style recipes).
  Users CANNOT create their own presets. That is the whitespace we fill.
- Soul ID = one-time TRAINED identity (20+ photos, 3-5 min, ~$1.25),
  then referenced by ID in every generation: "change only styling and
  setting in the prompt; leave the identity to Soul ID."
- Soul Cast = character sheet (front/side/back) generated BEFORE any
  scene; then every shot conditions on it. $5 to lock an actor.
- UGC Factory persists approved KEYFRAMES ("Image Stage") for reuse
  across sessions — their scale mechanism.
- NO series/episode object. NO multi-character locked identity. NO
  user-creatable templates. Their only cross-session continuity is
  bridge-frame chaining. Our server-side template store beats it.
- Their honest quality bar (their own blog): "clearly the same person,
  not pixel-identical"; identity drifts on WIDE shots.

INDUSTRY PATTERNS WE ADOPT:
- HeyGen: template = locked structure + typed variable slots; a new
  video = mail-merge (fill only the text slots). Voice is a SEPARATE
  typed slot from character.
- Kling Elements / Vidu My References: entities captured as 4-angle
  reference packs, FROZEN after save (versioned, never edited).
- LTX Studio Elements (our own model family): typed entities
  (character/location/prop/style/brand) + @-name mentions in prompts.
- Industry framing: "session memory" (95-98% consistent within a run,
  resets after) vs "character lock" (persisted artifacts, survives
  weeks). Studios on session-memory burn 20-40% of volume on retakes.
  Templates must store ENFORCED ARTIFACTS, never trust chat memory.
- NOBODY ships user-generated templates + an episode object. This is
  a real differentiation window, not a catch-up feature.

CONSISTENCY TECH RANKING (for our exact stack, A40 48GB):
  1. Qwen-Edit keyframe compositing onto STORED portraits + room
     plates, then Wan i2v  — zero training, everything already on pod.
  2. #1 + per-character Wan 2.2 LoRA (12-20 images, rank 16, overnight
     train via musubi-tuner/ai-toolkit) — strongest known combo;
     avatar_profiles.lora_path column already reserved for this.
  3. LTX IC-LoRA Ingredients — template-native (the sheet IS the
     template) but locked to 768x448 and flaky with 2-3 characters.
     Validate before trusting.
  4. Stand-In (face adapter) — best pure face sim, face-only, wrapper-
     dependent. Evaluation spike.
  5. Seeds are NOT a consistency mechanism (reproducibility only).
     Environment LoRAs are NOT needed (Wan generalizes rooms; plates
     + compositing carry them).
- Faces hold ~3-6s of moderate motion from a locked keyframe; keep
  identity beats close/mid; wide shots carried by the ROOM.

DIALOGUE (2-3 characters speaking):
- Production consensus (incl. Runway's own docs): ONE SPEAKER PER
  SHOT, assembled as shot/reverse-shot with gaze constraints (180
  rule). Even Veo 3 falls below 40% success on in-frame multi-speaker.
- Our per-segment voices already exist (sequence lipsync segments +
  duo lane). Planner will emit speaker tags + gaze/OTS constraints.
- True in-frame two-person: our duo (LongCat) lane is WIRED BUT NEVER
  PRODUCED VIDEO (outputs/duo/video is empty) — one validation test.
  InfiniteTalk-multi (Kijai wrapper) exists but ~30min/11s at 480p —
  garnish, never the workhorse.

2. WHAT WE ALREADY HAVE (codebase map — build on, don't rebuild)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- characters table (name + verbatim anchor + face + sheet + voice_id)
  = the embryonic entity store. avatars table = real-face presenters
  with consent. Both persist server-side in SQLite.
- inject_cast() = ONE injection seam all pipelines inherit.
- plan(cast=, script=, verbatim=) + _enforce_verbatim (code-level
  85%-word-survival repair) + async /plan already exist.
- PlanApproach JSON = the natural template payload (pipeline, shots,
  negatives, narration, needs_from_user).
- sequence mode = mixed-engine timeline with per-segment voice/image
  — a Template instance can COMPILE DOWN to a sequence request.
- fireApproach() in the timeline = proven coercion/asset-role wiring
  for instantiation. Reuse, don't reimplement.
- Keyframe machinery: generate_face / generate_sheet / derive_keyframe
  (2 refs: character + product) / derive_variants (4 emotions).
- Recipe + QC sidecars = audit-trail scaffolding, field-tolerant.
- wan_flf2v (first+last frame) mapping exists, UNWIRED — future
  continuity joins.

GAPS THE PLAN CLOSES (found by code recon):
- No template/environment/episode entity anywhere.
- Director cannot reference a character by name; frontend auto-injects
  ALL saved characters on every render; session.cast is a dead field.
- render_shot passes no character_ids (anchor text only).
- Create page's plan() doesn't send cast_ids (Director's does).
- Seeds NEVER persisted (recipe/qc sidecars lack them) — the winning
  look of a QC re-roll is unrecoverable today.
- QC vision judges receive TEXT ONLY — no reference image ever
  attached; adherence is asserted, not proven.
- characters store voice_id but NOT tts model_id/voice_settings —
  delivery can drift between episodes on a code-constant change.
- plan_dialogue verbatim has no code-level enforcement pass.
- negative_prompt is INERT on distilled LTX lanes — template schema
  must carry per-lane capability flags.

3. THE DATA MODEL (3 layers, all server-side SQLite,
   characters.py CRUD pattern — third/fourth tables)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

LAYER 1 — ENTITY LIBRARY (reusable across templates)
  characters (EXTEND existing):
    + turnaround sheet formalized as the 4-angle pack (front, 3/4 L,
      3/4 R, back — the Kling capture recipe)
    + emotion variant stills (existing derive_variants)
    + tts_model_id, tts_voice_settings columns (lock the DELIVERY,
      not just the voice id)
  environments (NEW table):
    id, name, verbatim setting-anchor text, 1-3 approved plates
    (wide / reverse / detail), created from a 1-frame Wan still or
    an upload, immutable after approval.
  (brand constants ride the template's look block + existing
   /endcard + /brand-pass endpoints)

LAYER 2 — templates (NEW table)
  - cast: 2-3 character_ids, each with locked voice (id + settings)
  - environments: environment_ids
  - look: frozen CHARACTER/SETTING/LOOK anchor blocks (verbatim),
    canonical negative block, grade line
  - grammar: beat-type -> pipeline lane routing, camera menu subset,
    knob defaults (quality/aspect/steps), duration target, language
  - keyframe bank: approved per-shot stills accumulate here across
    episodes (the Higgsfield Image Stage pattern)
  - calibration: identity/environment baseline scores measured at
    lock time (see §6)
  - per-lane capability flags (negatives inert on distilled LTX etc.)
  - status: draft -> validated -> LOCKED. Locked = immutable; edits
    create v2. (The Vidu freeze rule.)

LAYER 3 — episodes (NEW table)
  template_id + episode number + script (+ optional per-episode
  product image) + effective seeds + output paths + adherence scores.
  Ep2 sees Ep1's approved keyframes and continuity notes.

4. TEMPLATE CREATION — user-GENERATED, wizard flow
--------------------------------------------------------------------
User describes the show once ("young teacher, warm classroom,
playground outside, 6 students, bright optimistic grade, Hindi").
The planner proposes; the user approves each piece; nothing enters
the template unapproved. Steps:

  1. BRIEF -> planner proposes cast descriptions + rooms + look
     (editable cards, existing editable-plan UX)
  2. CAST: per character — generate hero portrait (existing
     generate_face) -> approve -> 4-angle sheet -> 4 emotion variants.
     Or pick an existing avatar/character. Assign voice per character
     (locked with settings).
  3. ROOMS: per environment — generate wide/reverse/detail plates
     (1-frame stills) -> approve. Or upload real location photos.
  4. LOOK: frozen anchor blocks + negative block, shown verbatim,
     editable before lock.
  5. VALIDATION BATCH (the gate Higgsfield doesn't have numbers for):
     8-12 test renders varying angle/lighting/beat type. Measured,
     not eyeballed — see §6. User reviews a grid, approves.
  6. LOCK. Template becomes immutable + versioned.

One-time cost per template: ~2.5-3.5 hr wall-clock incl. validation
on Wan (validation on LTX cuts it ~4x, ~35-50 min). Images are cheap
(~1-1.5 min/still); validation renders dominate.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
5. EPISODE RENDERING — the compiler
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Input: template_id + script. Everything else comes from the template.

  1. Script -> plan(verbatim=True, cast=template cast, constants=
     template look) -> beats with speaker tags. _enforce_verbatim
     repairs in code; NEW: same code-level pass added to the dialogue
     path (currently unguarded).
  2. KEYFRAME PASS: for each beat, Qwen-Edit composites the locked
     character portrait(s) + the locked environment plate into the
     shot keyframe. Identity + room locked at frame 1. Approved
     keyframes join the template's bank for reuse in later episodes.
  3. RENDER PASS, engine-batched (all Wan beats, then all LTX beats —
     measured model-swap penalty ~200s+ per Wan->LTX boundary, saves
     15-20 min/episode):
       - action/identity beats: Wan i2v from keyframe (3-6s, face
         large per planner rule)
       - speaking beats: per-speaker s2v/lipsync from that character's
         locked portrait + locked voice; shot/reverse-shot with gaze
         constraints; reaction-shot cutaways cover seams
       - wide establishing: carried by the room plate, faces small
       - b-roll/native-audio beats: LTX
  4. Dead-air guards + strict audio fit ride along as today.
  5. Seeds: effective per-shot seed (incl. QC take bump) + keyframe
     seeds PERSISTED into recipe.json. "The look that won" becomes
     reproducible for the first time.
  6. Brand pass: end card / captions via existing pod-free endpoints.

Per-episode cost (MEASURED, A40, QUALITY, QC on):
  - Wan beat: ~775s/take x 1.56 retry  (~20 min effective)
  - LTX beat: ~105s/take x 1.86        (~3.3 min effective)
  - CLIENT EPISODE LENGTH IS 1-2 MIN (~15-24 beats), so:
      all-Wan          = 4-6+ hr  -> NOT viable per episode
      smart mix (4 Wan hero beats + s2v speech + LTX rest)
                       = ~2.5-3 hr, ~$1.50-3 GPU
      LTX-heavy (Wan only 1-2 hero closeups)
                       = ~1-1.5 hr, ~$0.75-1.50
  - => 1-3 episodes/day on one A40. Beat routing is the biggest
    cost lever (6-8x spread). QUALITY s2v (>30min/take, never
    completed a measured run) is OFF the dialogue menu until
    benchmarked — use s2v FAST or LTX in-shot speech.
  - Volume beyond ~2 eps/day needs hardware (L40S ~1.5-2x, or a
    second pod fan-out), not software.
  - Promise SAME-DAY turnaround, not same-hour.
  - NOTE: Wan at 1280x704 has NEVER been rendered (all Wan history is
    640x640/432x768; every 1280x704 file is LTX) — benchmark on pod
    return before promising farmer-look at that res on Wan.

6. STRICTNESS — "strictly follow" made PROVABLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Today adherence is asserted (text-only VLM judges). We make it
measured, in three increments riding the existing QC seam
(_render_takes -> review_clip -> sidecar):

  A. SHIP FIRST (zero new dependencies):
     - Attach reference images to the vision judges: character
       portrait + environment plate alongside the 3 sampled frames
       ("same person as reference? same room? 0-100"). Payload-only
       change; the judge ladder already accepts multi-image.
     - ffmpeg SSIM of sampled frames vs the environment plate
       (matches qc.py's existing subprocess style).
     - Raise/restructure the 600-char context cap so anchors don't
       evict the shot brief.
     - Script verbatim check at generate time vs the template's
       canonical script (exact match at the TTS boundary).
     - All scores recorded per take in the qc sidecar = the audit
       trail we can show the client.
  B. LOCK-TIME CALIBRATION (what nobody in the market publishes):
     validation batch measures per-template baselines; episode gates
     are RELATIVE to baseline (robust to stylization), with take
     promotion preferring higher identity/env scores automatically
     (existing (ok, score) tuple).
  C. TIER-2 UPGRADE (when we want a number in the pitch deck):
     AuraFace-v1 embeddings — Apache 2.0, the ONLY clean commercial
     chain (insightface lib is MIT but buffalo_l/antelopev2 weights
     are NON-COMMERCIAL; AdaFace/facenet weights are dataset-tainted).
     CPU-only, ~100-200ms/frame, no GPU contention. Bands grounded in
     published FaceSim-Arc: pass >=0.5, warn 0.4-0.5, fail <0.4;
     DINOv2 cosine for rooms (~0.6-0.75 expected, cut set by
     calibration). Lets us say "identity verified via the standard
     FaceSim-Arc protocol" — a claim nobody else backs with numbers.

  Honest asymmetry, stated up front: voice lanes (lipsync/longcat/
  duo/redub) are QC-exempt by design (fixed-length, expensive takes)
  — there the gate VERIFIES AND WARNS, it does not re-roll.

9. HONEST LIMITS (say them to the client before they find them)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- "Same person" means unmistakably the same person, not pixel-
  identical — the whole industry's bar, including Higgsfield.
- Wide shots: the room carries the shot; faces soften. Identity
  beats stay close/mid by planner rule.
- Simultaneous in-frame two-person SPEECH is not in v1. Dialogue is
  cuts-based (shot/reverse-shot) — which is how real ads and TV cut
  dialogue anyway. The duo lane gets one validation test.
- A template guarantees its OWN look. A pod rebuild without P5's
  manifest can silently shift generation behavior (weights re-
  resolve from HF branch-latest today).
- Hindi/English word-rate sizing is estimated, not TTS-measured
  (existing disclosed deviation; assembly-time fit corrects).
