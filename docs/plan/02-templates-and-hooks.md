## Hook library

Sixteen hook types, each with authored Hinglish lines, the visual to pair in the first two seconds and the categories it fits; Script Brain must pick from at least three types for its six candidates, and a hook is never a product description.

**What the data says about the first 3 seconds.** Reels is more than half of Instagram time in India; Meta's own Reels research found direct-response ads 1.5× likelier to rank top-20 % for purchase intent with a visual and audio hook, 2.7× when the product is shown more than once, 4.8× when brand presence stays under a quarter of the runtime, and 1.9× with a CTA ([Meta–Toluna via Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/meta-playbook-boost-reels-ad-performance-10867111)). Third-party Reels benchmarks put the median hook rate near 30 % and hold rate near 23 %; treat 30 % hook and 45 % hold as the scale threshold and 25 % hook as the fix-the-first-2-seconds floor ([jetfuel 2026 benchmarks](https://jetfuel.agency/creative-performance-metrics-for-dtc-video-ads-hold-rate-thumbstop-and-thruplay-benchmarks-2026/), [AdLibrary Reels guide](https://adlibrary.com/posts/meta-reels-ads-guide-2026)). Reels plays with sound on for about 80 % of viewers, so the spoken hook matters as much as the text card ([Instagram stats](https://adamconnell.me/instagram-reels-statistics/)).

**Hook hygiene Script Brain enforces** ([TrueFan](https://www.truefan.ai/blogs/instagram-reels-hooks-india), [AlphaBeta](https://alphabetasolution.org/the-reels-playbook-for-indian-brands-in-2026-from-someone-whos-tested-everything/)): no logo intro, no "hi everyone", no landscape opener; the spoken hook lands inside 2 s and the text card at frame 1; at least one cut or motion event in the first second and three cuts in the first three; the product or the outcome is visible by second 3; the subject stays centred so budget-phone crops keep it; a trending or licensed sound is never used in a paid ad, so the sound hook is an ElevenLabs SFX hit on cut 1 ([Benly](https://benly.ai/learn/meta-ads/meta-ads-reels-ads-guide)).

**Scoring (1–5 each).** Clarity (understood without the picture), specificity (a number, a name, a place), curiosity (opens a loop the ad closes), naturalness (sounds like a phone call, not a brochure), category fit, length (≤ 8 spoken words). The three highest totals are shown; ties go to the type the brand has not used in its last three ads.

## Template library

Nineteen templates, every one a mapping onto the five beats with a 15 s and a 30 s beat sheet; the "UGC testimonial" of v1 is gone, replaced by a labelled presenter-explainer and a real-testimonial template, because ASCI's synthetic-content guidelines prohibit fabricated testimonials even when labelled ([ASCI press release, 29 Sep 2026](https://www.ascionline.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Press-Release_Guidelines-for-Responsible-Labelling-of-Synthetically-Generated-Content-in-Advertising.pdf)).

Word budgets assume 2.2–2.6 Hindi words per second (Indian 30 s TV ads average about 65 spoken words, [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/opinion/how-many-words-can-you-pack-in-an-ad-122030801615_1.html)); the measured voice always wins. "Lane" names the Shot Engine path: **Presenter** (Brand Face on screen, VO-led), **Product** (no people), **Re-shoot** (Genjutsu over a master template video), **Film** (one Cinema Studio take).

**Rules across templates**

- Brand presence (logo, pack-shot-only frames) stays under a quarter of the runtime; the product itself can be everywhere.
- Every template ends on the end card for 2–3 s with the CTA channel and the number inside the safe zone.
- A disclaimer, when a claim needs one, is one line, same language as the claim, on screen for at least 4 s under the CTA (ASCI disclaimer guidelines).
- Refresh: Script Brain offers a new hook for the same ad after 5–7 days in a festival window and 10–14 days otherwise, since frequency above 3–4 a week drops CTR 20–30 % in agency data ([AdsSarthi](https://adssarthi.com/blog/instagram-reels-ads-india-guide.html)).
