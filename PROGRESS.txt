 1 · B-ROLL PIPELINE  (Wan 2.2 t2v · mode "overlay")
================================================================================
WHAT IT DOES  Pure text-to-video scenes (~5s per shot), stitched, narration
              overlaid, optional music bed ducked underneath.

PROBLEM 1: Robotic narration ("audio in broll felt very robotic")
  CAUSE     ElevenLabs was called with NO voice_settings -> flat monotone read,
            and Gemini wrote choppy "Fragment. Fragment: list." style copy.
  FIX       (a) Expressive defaults sent on every TTS call:
                stability 0.4 · similarity_boost 0.75 · style 0.35 · speaker_boost on
            (b) Planner rule: scripts must be natural spoken sentences — never
                colon-fragment constructions. Verified by A/B revoicing the same clip.

PROBLEM 2: Audio/video sync slop (voice ends ~1s before video, or gets cut off)
  CAUSE     Video length is fixed by shot count; narration length is whatever
            ElevenLabs produces. Old assembly (apad + -shortest) never fit either
            side to the other.
  FIX       Auto-fit at assembly (single FFmpeg pass, all future renders):
              voice shorter  -> video trimmed to voice-end + 0.45s breathing tail
              voice longer   -> sped up to at most 1.12x (imperceptible); beyond
                                that: kept + warning in job detail ("shorten script")
            PLUS the user-facing "✂ Fix timing" tool in Library for old videos:
              auto-trim dead tail (silence detection; incl. fix for ffmpeg
              closing the last silence interval at EOF) · tail choice 0.2/0.45/0.8s
              · manual "cut at N s". Runs locally in seconds, no pod.

PROBLEM 3: "I don't see b-roll video with audio!"
  CAUSE     Music/narration lane gains + a stitched intermediate being surfaced.
  FIX       Gain chain verified (music ducked to 15% under voice; measured
            -17 dB on the battery-F clip), intermediates deleted after assembly
            so only the finished final lands in the Library.

PROBLEM 4: Stitching clips of different resolutions hard-failed
  FIX       Every lane normalized (scale + pad + fps + SAR to the first clip)
            and silent clips get a generated silent audio lane, so any mix of
            clips concatenates cleanly (this is also what powers Remix).

TIMINGS (measured on the render battery)
  Preview, cold pod (models loading)      4 min 45 s   (1 shot + TTS + assembly)
  Preview, warm pod re-roll               1 min 45 s
  Moderate (preview + polish chain)       ~5 min render + ~10 min enhance
                                          -> proof: 864x1536 @ 32fps output
  TTS                                     a few seconds
  Assembly / revoice / fix-timing         seconds (local FFmpeg, no pod)

 2 · PRODUCT PIPELINE  (Wan 2.2 i2v · mode "product")
================================================================================
WHAT IT DOES  Animates YOUR product photo with cinematic camera moves — the
              photo locks the product's exact look (no AI hallucinated bottle).

PROBLEM 1: Uploads didn't work in the old UI
  FIX       Dropzone -> backend /assets upload -> pod image upload; the image is
            uploaded to the pod ONCE and cached even when several segments
            reuse it (sequence mode).

PROBLEM 2: Enhance ruined product shots
  CAUSE     The polish chain starts with CodeFormer FACE restoration — products
            have no faces; it "restored" label text into mush.
  FIX       CodeFormer is skipped automatically for product renders (both the
            auto chain and the Library Enhance button).

PROBLEM 3: Same sync/robotic-voice issues as B-roll
  FIX       Shared: auto-fit + expressive TTS apply to product finals too.

TIMINGS
  Preview                                 ~2 min per shot (+ seconds TTS/assembly)
  Moderate                                + ~10 min polish chain
  Master (20 steps, never run yet)        long render — first run pending

 3 · AVATAR PIPELINE  (Wan-S2V lipsync · mode "lipsync")
================================================================================
WHAT IT DOES  A spokesperson (from ONE face image) reads your script, lips
              synced to the ElevenLabs voice. Audio-first: the voice track
              drives the video, so this pipeline is exempt from auto-fit.

PROBLEM 1: Hindi lipsync ("wav2vec2 speech encoder is English-only")
  STATUS    Tested Hindi anyway (battery D): 14.9s output — you LIKED it.
            Queued (your call): compare a multilingual wav2vec2 encoder vs the
            LongCat-Video-Avatar 1.5 route before investing.

PROBLEM 2: Editing the voice of an avatar video would desync the mouth
  FIX       Voice-lock system: avatar renders (and any sequence/remix final
            containing an avatar scene, tracked via sidecar files) are BLOCKED
            from revoice/re-narration — backend refuses (422) and the UI
            explains + offers "re-render with a new voice" instead.

PROBLEM 3: Quality preset nuance
  FIX       Fast Lightning LoRA is used for preview, but QUALITY mode switches
            s2v to a proper 20-step render with the LoRA off (LoRA hurts s2v
            at high step counts).

TIMINGS
  Preview                                 ~6 min for the ~14.4s fixed take
  Master                                  ~35 min + polish (not yet run)
  Output length                           ~14.4s per take (model constraint)

 CROSS-PIPELINE FIXES (apply to all three + sequence/remix)

    pod-busy line (built for the multi-pod future).

    seconds with no pod (voice-lock rules enforced).
  · Vercel 404 fixed (monorepo root vercel.json) + full mobile responsiveness
    (sidebar -> top bar + drawer on phones) + landing-first routing.
